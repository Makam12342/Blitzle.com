Blitzle

Welcome to Blitzle.com, a fun chess game where you can play against friends in school without having the wifi blocked. In Blitzle.com you can play three different game modes Online, Local Play, and Against AI. In Blitzle.com the main concept is fast paced chess games where time is your enemy. To be good at chess is one thing but to be good at chess with a timer chasing you every waking second is a whole nother story. DO you think you have what it takes to be the blitzle master?

Main Discription

To play Blitzle there are three game modes which are all described below. This website has a sidebar located to the left that navigates you to all the pages you need. When you click on the sidebar buttons it will link you to the given pages. If you look to the bottom of the sidebar you will see a small chat section so you can talk to friends while you play. An important thing to consider when using this section is that your chat messages are not private and are broadcasted to everyone who is online at the moment. If you ever get stuck and don't know how to get home just click on the Blitzle logo and it will link you back to the homepage. When playing on Blitzle I ask that you be respectful to other players and that the chat should not be used for any kind of harassment. "

Local Play

Local play is a simple gamemode where you can play against your friends on a local device the gamemode can be accessed from the side bar. When playing, a timer and move history section can be found on the right of the chess board.

Play Online

Live play is a fun online gamemode where you can play against friends in different classes. To play against a friend you simply need to create a room with a room name and username of your choice. Once the room is created it will show up on the join section where you and your friend both just join the room. When you guys are both in the room white will be able to move with the timers starting after white's first move to the right you will see the move history and timers for the game.

Vs computer

AI play is a simple gamemode where you put your skills up against a coded algorithm and try to beat it. It has the same rules and local play but your opponent is instead an AI algorithm trained to make the best moves.
